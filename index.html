Common questions

My styled code won't scan. Check the "Scans well" badge under the preview in the designer. If it shows a warning, add contrast between the code and its background, make the logo smaller, or pick simpler dot and eye shapes. Always test a printed proof with more than one phone.

I forgot the password of a locked note. It can't be recovered. The password is never stored, so nobody, including us, can open the note without it.

How do I move my codes to a new phone? Your library is part of your normal iPhone backup, so restoring a backup brings it back. You can also export your history as a spreadsheet from Settings.
